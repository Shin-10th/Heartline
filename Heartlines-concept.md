# Heartlines
### *Two hearts, one line.*

A concept for an app built specifically for long-distance couples — less a messaging app, more a small shared ritual space that quietly handles timezones, sends reminders that feel like affection instead of admin, and turns short voice clips into something a bit more personal than a text.

---

## The idea in one line

Most LDR communication happens through generic tools (WhatsApp, a shared calendar, a countdown app) that weren't built for the specific ache of being on different clocks. Heartlines bundles the four things that actually matter — *when to talk, how to be reminded, how to send a little bit of your voice, and the small rituals that hold a relationship together across distance* — into one warm, simple app.

## Core features

**Golden Hour scheduling.** Heartlines knows both of your timezones and quietly finds your overlapping "golden window" — the stretch of the day where you're both free and awake. Instead of doing timezone math, you get a suggested slot ("8:00 PM your time / 12:00 PM theirs") with one tap to lock it in. Both of you get a gentle reminder 10 minutes before.

**Voice notes that become ringtones.** Send short voice clips back and forth like little gifts, not chat messages. The best ones can be set as your partner's custom ringtone or notification sound for you — so when they call, they hear your actual voice before they even pick up.

**Little rituals, with streaks.** Recurring, low-effort touchpoints you set up once and keep without thinking: a good-morning ping, a goodnight message, a weekly date night, a daily photo of your day. Each one tracks a gentle streak (not guilt-inducing, just a nice number that goes up) and can be paused any time.

**"Send a heartbeat."** One tap sends a soft vibration/notification to your partner's phone — no words needed. It's for the moments you're thinking of them but don't have anything to say.

**The distance, made visible.** A simple home screen shows both your local times side by side, the physical distance between you right now, and a countdown to your next visit — pulled from a saved flight or trip date.

**Pixel selves & your World.** Upload a photo of each of you and Heartlines turns it into a small, cute pixel-art character of you — used as a little mascot throughout the app (on the home screen, celebrating a streak) and, more fully, living in a shared "World" screen: a tiny cozy pixel room where your two avatars hang out together. The room gets new decor (fairy lights, a rug, a bookshelf, a star jar) as your rituals and streaks grow, and you can send each other small in-room "gifts" — a light gamification layer that turns consistency into something visual and a little playful, instead of just a number going up.

*How the real version would work:* the actual photo → pixel-avatar step needs an image-generation/style-transfer model — something that reads the photo (hair color, skin tone, glasses, hairstyle) and renders a small low-res "sprite" in a consistent art style, similar to how apps generate cartoon avatars from selfies today. That's a backend AI feature to build and isn't something a static design mockup can do, so the mockup shows generic placeholder avatars (pink = you, lavender = your partner) standing in for what a real generated character would look like.

## Tone & design direction

Extra cute and lovely: a soft cream-pink background with bubblegum pink and lavender accents, rounded playful type (Fredoka for headlines, Quicksand for everything else), and hand-drawn little hearts sprinkled throughout — dotting the wordmark, trailing between your two cities on the map, floating up from the voice recorder. No cats, no stock emoji — the cuteness comes from small hand-drawn details and warm language, not clip art.

## What I made alongside this

I put together a UI mockup of five core screens — Home, Schedule ("Golden Hour"), Voice notes, Rituals, and World (the pixel-avatar room) — as a visual design canvas so you can see how this would actually look and feel before anyone writes a line of code. I sent that as a separate design over in the conversation, and it's already been updated to this name, theme, and the new World screen.

## Names considered along the way

- **Heartlines** *(chosen)* — a heartline and a phone line between two people, all in one word
- Heartspan — poetic runner-up, like "wingspan" for how far a heart can reach
- SameSky — the original pick; different places, same sky above you both
- Smitten, Loveloop, Squish, Dote, Farhug — other directions explored (playful, poetic, and pun-forward) before settling on Heartlines
